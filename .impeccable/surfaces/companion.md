# Companion app (app/index.html)

Mode: Operate. Phone first, also the Mac window.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A companion that feels like a first-party Apple app for one robot: the conversation is the screen, everything else is a sheet away. Refuses the dashboard of cards, meters and toggles the old app was.

OWN-WORLD: Apple's system grammar (large title, grouped inset lists, sheet, toolbar material, SF text) in Brandon's palette: desk-grey ground, ink-navy ink and user bubbles, kraft as BITCH's colour. Helvetica Neue for the large title. Light and dark follow the system.

STORY: Brandon glances to see what BITCH is doing, reads what it heard and said, types when voice is awkward, mutes his mic or BITCH, stops it. Settings when he needs a device or the camera.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with BITCH's googly eyes and name at left, voice mute and Settings at right; one status line under it; conversation fills the middle, newest at the bottom; bottom toolbar with mic mute, message field, send. Stop appears beside the status only while BITCH talks or performs.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (Apple app grammar); seed: none (brief-pinned). Signature move: the googly eyes, alive to BITCH's state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
